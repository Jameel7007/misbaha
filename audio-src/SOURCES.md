# Sound sources

The bead clicks are cut from these recordings on [Freesound](https://freesound.org) by
`npm run build:clicks` (`scripts/build-clicks.mjs`), which writes `src/clicks.data.js`.
The files here are Freesound's high-quality MP3 previews.

| File | Recording | Author | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `347928-rosary.mp3` | [rosary.wav](https://freesound.org/people/jaset13045/sounds/347928/) | jaset13045 | CC0 | amber |
| `614852-wooden-bracelet.mp3` | [Wooden Bead Bracelet fiddling & Quiet fabric movement](https://freesound.org/people/NichelleMedia/sounds/614852/) | NichelleMedia | CC0 | olive |
| `174216-abacus-hard.mp3` | [essen_mysounds_chiara.wav](https://freesound.org/people/thecityrings/sounds/174216/) | thecityrings | [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) | ebony; the contact of the brass separators |
| `863239-abacus.mp3` | [Abacus (SoundAction 261)](https://freesound.org/people/alexarje/sounds/863239/) | alexarje | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | ebony |
| `483451-worry-beads.mp3` | [Worry Beads.wav](https://freesound.org/people/PappaBert/sounds/483451/) | PappaBert | CC0 | not used: a continuous rattle, no single clicks |

The CC BY recordings are credited on the page, in About. The clicks were trimmed,
high-passed at 80 Hz, faded and levelled; nothing else was changed.

`preview.wav` (not committed) is every chosen click in order, for listening.
