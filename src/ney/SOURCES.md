# The ney recordings

The ambience plays phrases from these recordings on [Freesound](https://freesound.org)
(their high-quality MP3 previews). `npm run find:phrases` (`scripts/find-phrases.mjs`)
finds the phrases and writes `phrases.js`.

| File | Recording | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `115398-ney-istanbul.mp3` | [ney.wav](https://freesound.org/people/xserra/sounds/115398/): played by Hamza Zeytinoğlu in Istanbul | xserra | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `440816-ney.mp3` | [ney-01.wav](https://freesound.org/people/avnonsen/sounds/440816/) | avnonsen | CC0 |
| `580759-ney-ankara.mp3` | [Ankara-110-Amin - Ney.wav](https://freesound.org/people/aliaref/sounds/580759/) | aliaref | CC0 |
| `402709-arab-flute.mp3` | [Arab flute.mp3](https://freesound.org/people/KasDonatov/sounds/402709/) | KasDonatov | CC0 |

The CC BY recording is credited on the page, in About. Nothing in the recordings was
changed; the page only chooses where each phrase starts and ends, fades it, sets its
loudness and adds reverb.
